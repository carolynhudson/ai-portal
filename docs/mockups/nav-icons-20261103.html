Mockup only (2026-11-03). Halo-mark colors — blue #5aa9ff → violet #bc8cff ring gradient, amber #d29922 as the third voice. No words in any icon; labels live beside/below icons, never inside. Color law (owner): the mark's blue→violet is the base for every icon. Colors outside the mark — ok #3fb950, warn #d29922 — enter sparingly, small area, only to differentiate or draw attention: the green in Health's heart (it is the page with a status light), amber only on the workbench voices (wrench, gear core, llama's band, the model sparks). Same discipline as the hero's start/stop/restart buttons and the warning lamps: the accent means something, so it must stay rare. Red is never a nav icon — bad is a state, not a destination.

Touch-nav practice, in one page:
  • Nested submenus are the touch sin. Hover doesn't exist on a phone; flyout children miss under the thumb. Accepted pattern: a tap opens a single-level sheet; deeper navigation is a new sheet with a back row, never a child list.
  • 44×44px minimum target (Apple HIG) with real gaps between — this bar uses 44px+ targets and 8px+ spacing.
  • The sheet must end on screen — no scrolling menu. Seven top icons and three sheet rows both fit at 390px.
  • Recognition beats recall: an icon row shows the whole site; a burger hides it behind a memory test. The burger earns its place only past ~5–7 top targets — that's the "More" (three dots) slot, not the whole nav.
  • Icons without words cost legibility (yours: "harder to make understandable at a glance") — mitigated by strong silhouettes, the active icon lit with a tinted backing, and the page title below. Ship the icon bar with words on desktop, icons-only (or icon+micro-label) on phone.
  • Sheets are bottom-anchored: the thumb's natural arc. Top-drawer menus are for the thumb's reach limit.

    Notes for the real round

  • Icons are static SVGs (no animation, no glow beyond the theme gradients) so they inherit the portal's quiet tone; the active page's icon gets the tinted backing plate, which is a state, not decoration.
  • Accessibility: every icon is an <a> with aria-label + title (the words exist for readers/screen readers, they just don't render inside the glyph). The probe's tap-target check (≥44px) is the acceptance gate.
  • Sub-sheets replace today's in-page tabs as entry points — the Models page keeps its tabs; the sheet just lands you on the right tab (/models?tab=search). Personalities sheet = two existing routes. No new machinery; the viewport probe's "overflow 0 at 390px" is the acceptance test the nav round must pass.
  • Overflow slot ("More") exists in the bar for future sections so the bar never needs a burger for one new page.
